When photography, design and technology come together, there are more ways to tell the story of an idea. In this article, I would like to introduce **Luce Creative Studio**, where I bring my work in these fields together.

Luce covers everything from a brand’s digital presence to the way it explains its products. A website, a product photograph, an exhibition stand or an interactive simulation can answer different needs and complement one another.

**[Visit Luce Creative Studio →](https://luce.tr/en/)**

## What kind of work does Luce do?

The studio’s services fall into several areas:

- **3D modelling and design:** Product display stands, interiors and exhibition stands; visualising an idea before it is built.
- **Interactive simulations:** Exploring how a product, machine or concept works through a browser-based experience.
- **Web design and SEO:** Websites that explain services clearly, alongside work on search visibility.
- **AI automation:** Customer communication and workflows considered around the needs of each business.
- **Graphic design and social media:** Brand identity, visual content and digital communication.
- **Photography and promotional video:** Product, interior, corporate, event and special-occasion shoots.

The scope varies from one project to another. Before choosing a tool, the starting point is what we need to explain and who we want to reach.

[Explore all services at Luce.](https://luce.tr/en/services/)

## Where design becomes an experience

One area that particularly interests me is combining visual storytelling with interaction. Instead of showing a product from a single angle, we can turn it into an application where visitors inspect its parts and try its controls.

Luce’s camera and torque simulators are two examples. One lets you see how aperture, shutter speed and ISO affect a photograph; the other lets you explore balance by changing masses and lever arms.

**[Open the Luce simulator hub →](https://luce.tr/en/simulator/)**

These examples also show how a simulator can be designed around different needs. Whether the purpose is education, presentation or explaining a working principle, it helps to identify the behaviour first and build the interaction around it.

## How are Sezgin Mesut and Luce connected?

On this site, I share experiences connected to my personal interests: travel, photography, gardening and workshop projects. Luce brings design and digital production services, project examples and interactive applications together under a separate name.

What connects the two is learning by making and trying things. Exploring a project, developing a visual idea or making a subject easier to understand are all part of that approach.
