Hello, I’m **Sezgin Mesut**. I work on photography, design and digital content. I enjoy trying something new, understanding how it works and sharing what I learn. Being behind a camera, working on a piece of wood or heading out on a motorcycle are different expressions of that curiosity.

**sezginmesut.com** is my personal website, bringing my work and content together under my own name. Here you can read my articles, find my video channels and explore my photography and design portfolios.

## Photography and design

For me, photography is both a way to tell stories and a reason to look more closely at my surroundings. From scenes encountered while travelling to wedding photographs in my portfolio, I am interested in the story an image can carry.

You can see my wedding photography in my [MyWed portfolio](https://mywed.com/tr/photographer/sezginmesut/), browse my stock photographs on [Shutterstock](https://www.shutterstock.com/tr/g/sezginmesut), and explore my design work on [Behance](https://www.behance.net/sezginmesut).

## Content built around hands-on experience

The articles on this site grow from things I have tried and experiences I have had:

- **Photography and product experiences:** I discuss tools and products through my own use of them.
- **Travel and motorcycles:** I share stories from the road, journeys and situations I encounter.
- **Gardening:** I document soil preparation, composting and growing experiments.
- **Woodworking:** I keep notes on hand tools, carving and DIY projects.

For example, you can read about my [Istanbul–Bursa trip on a Honda SH125](/en/blog/istanbul-bursa-honda-sh125/), [building a Hügelkultur garden bed](/en/blog/building-my-hugelkultur-garden-bed/), and [comparing a Dremel with carving chisels](/en/blog/dremel-or-carving-chisels/).

## Luce Creative Studio and my digital projects

I bring my design, photography and digital development work together through **[Luce Creative Studio](https://luce.tr/en/)**. Alongside web design, 3D modelling and visual storytelling, Luce includes interactive applications.

**[Sen Pişir](https://senpisir.tr/)** is one of my projects turning an everyday need into a digital tool: it helps people find meals using ingredients at home and decide what to cook. I introduce the website and Android app in my [Sen Pişir article](/en/blog/sen-pisir-ingredient-based-meal-ideas/).

The [Luce simulators](/en/blog/luce-camera-torque-simulators/), which help users explore camera settings and the relationship between torque and equilibrium, are another example of combining visual explanation with interaction.
